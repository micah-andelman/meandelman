Skip the 3D tour

A personal agent I text on Telegram. It is Claude Code running headless on a server, with tools I wrote for my calendar, email, Canvas, grades and the campus buses. It messages me every morning and evening, and sends a longer review on Sundays.

Most of my time went into the tools. Each one does one job and says so when it is not sure of something, so a guessed exam date or a room it could not find is pointed out in the answer instead of being filled in. It only suggests a bus when the bus beats walking by at least five minutes.

Pluto 7:02 AM

Morning. Two classes today: ENES100 at 10:00 and Calc III at 2:00.

Your physics problem set is due at 11:59 PM and isn't on your calendar, so I blocked 4:00 to 5:30.

Bus 104 leaves in 6 minutes, but walking to class is faster today.

Pluto 3:41 PM

When's my next Calc III exam, where is it, and what do I need on it to keep an A?

Exam 2 is Thursday at 2:00. That date is from the syllabus on Canvas, not your calendar, so check it in class.

I couldn't find a room for it. Canvas and the schedule don't list one yet.

To stay at an A you need about an 84 on it, assuming the final is weighted the way the syllabus says.

Pluto

Claude Code, headless, on Fly.io
Claude Code runs headless on my subscription with the official Telegram channel plugin. When I hit the subscription limit it switches to an API key, and it switches back once the limit resets.

Its tools

Calendar
Creates, moves and searches events, and finds free time between classes.
Email
It sorts my inbox and sends digests. It also pulls dates out of emails, so a rescheduled exam or an extension that never made it onto my calendar still shows up.
Canvas sync
Every six hours it copies new Canvas assignments onto my calendar, moves any whose deadline changed, and sends me one summary.
Grade projection
Works out my grade by category weight, leaving out categories with nothing graded yet, and tells me what I need on the rest of the work.
Study planner
Fits study blocks into the free time on my calendar, up to a daily limit. If something does not fit, it tells me.
Campus buses
Compares walking, biking and the Shuttle-UM buses, using live bus positions instead of the posted schedule.
Campus map
Looks up building codes and coordinates from UMD's facilities map. If a name could mean two buildings, it returns both.
